Hi everyone!

SpareForge is now playable on itch.io. It's an action RPG in the spirit of the classic online RPGs: grind levels, change jobs, hunt for rare drops and get your favourite weapon refined to +10 at the blacksmith. It all happens in real time, offline on your own PC, solo or with up to three friends over LAN.

## What's already in

- **20 regions** from level 1 to 150, from the meadows around Forge Town to the Void Sanctum
- **6 classes and 6 advanced jobs** with over 80 skills
- **Loot in 6 rarity tiers**, cards, visible gear, refining up to +10, durability and repairs
- **Field bosses, dungeon bosses and MVPs**
- **Pets** that pick up loot for you and **mounts** from a pony to a gryphon
- **A main story** in 10 chapters, plus side quests
- **A hand-painted world map** with waypoints
- **LAN co-op for up to 4 players** with a party, trading, chat and arena duels

## Why alpha?

You can play the game from start to finish, but it's far from done. Balance, content and visuals are still changing, and there will be bugs. Your characters normally carry over to every update. If a save reset ever becomes necessary during the alpha, I'll announce it in advance.

## Your feedback

Your feedback helps more than anything: what's fun, what's annoying, what's missing? Report bugs and share ideas on [Discord](https://discord.gg/wkMzgF4yV9) or here in the comments. You'll also find news on **spareforge.net**.

Have fun forging your legend!

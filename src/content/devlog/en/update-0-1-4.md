Hi everyone!

Update 0.1.4 is out. It's all about loot: rare drops now roll random affixes, the blacksmith can reforge them, casters finally get cast speed, and the shops are a lot easier to browse.

## Random prefixes and suffixes

Every weapon and armour piece of **rare rarity or better and level 15+** now drops with random affixes – one to three prefixes and one to three suffixes.

| Rarity | Affixes |
|---|---|
| Rare | 2–4 |
| Epic | 3–5 |
| Legendary | 4–6 |
| Unique | 5–6 |

Each line rolls a quality tier: **Crude, Common, Fine, Superb or Perfect** – and only 2% of all lines come out Perfect. The item name shows the first prefix and suffix, e.g. *Mighty Raven Gloves of Haste*, and the tooltip lists every line with its tier. Prefixes bring main stats, ATK, MATK or Max HP; suffixes bring HIT, FLEE, CRIT, ASPD, DEF, MDEF, Max SP or move speed. Affixes count in the item comparison and stay on the item through trades, storage, pet bags, ground drops and co-op.

## Reforging at the blacksmith

The blacksmith has a new **Reforge** tab:

- **Reroll values** – keep your affixes, roll new tiers
- **Reroll line** – pick one line and turn it into another affix of the same kind
- **Reroll all** – a completely fresh roll

Every roll costs zeny plus refine materials of the item's tier. A perfect line takes about 50 tries on average, so a fully perfect item is a true endgame goal. Rare gear you found before this update has no affixes yet – *Reroll all* gives it a set.

## Cast speed

Mages, wizards, acolytes and priests finally get **cast speed**. It only appears on caster weapons (staves and acolyte maces) and on mage / acolyte shields – never on helmets, armour, boots, gloves or accessories. You'll find it as a base bonus on the item and on the new *Chanting* prefix and *of Casting* suffix.

Below base level 100 cast speed is capped at 90%. From **level 100**, 100% cast speed means **instant casts** – but you'll need level 100+ gear with near-perfect rolls to get there. Your current cast speed is shown as **CAST** in the status window.

## Gloves replace cloaks

The cloak slot is now a **gloves** slot. All 22 cloaks, capes and mantles became gloves, gauntlets, mittens and wraps with new icons and gloves-style bonuses (HIT, DEX, STR, CRIT). The former cloak cards now slot into gloves. **Your saves are converted automatically** – a worn cloak becomes the matching gloves, refine level and card included.

## Shop tabs

Every shop – including the job vendors – now has category tabs: **Weapons** (with a second row per weapon type), **Shields, Headgear, Armor, Gloves, Shoes, Accessories, Potions & Food, Arrows and Materials**. The tabs also filter the Sell list.

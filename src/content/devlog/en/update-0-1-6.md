Hi everyone!

Update 0.1.6 is out. This one is about how the game *feels*: Warriors and Vanguards are real front-liners now, loot picks itself up if you want it to, the shops and stat buttons save you a lot of clicking, monsters no longer get stuck behind a rock, refined weapons glow, and a few annoying bugs are gone.

## Warrior and Vanguard tank pass

The two melee tank jobs finally behave like tanks.

- **More HP:** Warrior HP growth goes up from 1.8 to 2.5, and the Vanguard now has 3.2.
- **Innate defence:** both jobs gain DEF with every base level (Warrior +0.2, Vanguard +0.3 per level).
- **Skills:**

| Skill | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Blade Discipline | +4 ATK per level | +6 ATK per level |
| Iron Constitution | +8 max HP per level | +30 max HP and +2 DEF per level |
| Crushing Blow | 130% – 400% ATK | 160% – 430% ATK |
| Rending Thrust | 150% ATK, +30% per level | 180% ATK, +30% per level |
| Cinder Burst | 140% – 320% ATK | 170% – 350% ATK |
| Lance Doctrine (Vanguard) | +5 ATK per level | +8 ATK per level |
| Valor Oath (Vanguard) | DEF 5 – 25 | DEF 10 – 50 |

## Auto-loot

Press **V** to toggle auto-loot. While it is on, your character picks up every drop within pickup range by itself – no more Space-mashing after a fight. The setting is remembered between sessions.

## Smarter monsters

Chasing monsters now **path around obstacles** (walls, rocks and so on) instead of pushing against them. In open fields nothing changes, so the cheap direct chase stays.

## Less clicking

- **Hold to spend stat points:** hold the **+** button in the status window and it keeps adding points.
- **Shop "Max" button:** buy as many as your crowns allow, or sell a whole stack with one click.
- **Pet tab:** with a grab pet equipped, the inventory has a new **Pet** tab that opens and closes your pet's bag.
- **Buy back:** every shop now has a **Buy Back** tab. Sold something by accident? Your last 20 sales can be bought back for the same price, and gear comes back exactly as it was – refine, cards and durability included.
- **One talk, one quest:** accepting a quest now closes the window instead of offering the next one right away. What you accepted is shown in the message log; talk to the NPC again for the next quest.

## Refined weapons and shields glow

Your refine level now shows on your character:

- **+3:** a faint, bright shimmer
- **+5:** a clear bluish halo
- **+7:** a golden halo with a streak of light running along the blade
- **+10:** the "excellent" glow – shifting rainbow colours, a fast sheen and sparkles

Your co-op partners see the glow too, of course.

## Gear wears slower

Equipment durability was draining too fast, so wear is now five times lower: weapons lose 0.12 % per hit instead of 0.6 %, and armour loses 0.04 % per hit taken instead of 0.2 %. You will need the blacksmith's repair service a lot less often.

## Bug fixes

- **No more damage after teleporting:** if you were being chased and teleported to town, you sometimes kept taking damage – mostly in co-op, where maps you left keep running in the background. Monsters now only attack players on their own map, and you are protected from hits for 1.5 seconds after every map change.
- **Grab pets in a party:** your pet used to pick up loot reserved for other party members. Now it only collects what belongs to you or to nobody any more.

Hi everyone!

Update 0.1.7 is out. The focus is on fairness between classes, a way to change your mind about your class, and a handful of quality-of-life changes in the shop and the inventory.

## Class balance

All classes now deal similar damage, and none of them is overpowered any more. Supports keep their role: they heal and buff the party instead of competing on raw damage.

## +20 % base EXP

Base EXP is up by 20 %, so levelling takes a bit less grinding.

## Class change at Tutor Wennick

Picked the wrong class? Tutor Wennick now offers a **paid class change**, so you can switch without starting over.

## Shop and inventory

- **Confirm dialog:** the shop asks you to confirm before it completes a purchase or sale.
- **Instant quantity input:** type the amount straight away, no extra click needed.
- **Docked equipment:** the equipment window is now attached to the inventory. Drag an item onto it to equip it.

## Translations and fixes

The 12 game languages received updated translations, and a number of smaller bugs are fixed.

## Co-op

Everyone in the party has to be on **0.1.7**. Players on different versions cannot play together, so make sure your friends update too.

## Your feedback

Do the classes feel balanced now? Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/wkMzgF4yV9) or on **spareforge.net**.

SpareForge is still in Alpha, so expect bugs and balance changes, and please back up your saves.

Happy hunting!
